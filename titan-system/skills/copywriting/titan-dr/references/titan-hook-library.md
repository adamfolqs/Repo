# TITAN HOOK LIBRARY
## Built from analysis of 6,812 DTC ads across 84 verified brands (v5 dataset — health, supplement, wellness, beauty, fitness)

> A hook has ONE job: stop the scroll and create a reason to keep reading/watching.
> The best hooks work on ONE of three levers: CURIOSITY, FEAR, or DESIRE.
> Every hook type below maps to one or more of these levers.

---

## HOOK TAXONOMY

---

### HOOK TYPE 1: CONTRARIAN / PATTERN INTERRUPT
**Lever:** Curiosity + Desire
**What it does:** Contradicts what the reader assumes is the only option. Opens by rejecting the mainstream solution before the reader has a chance to tune out. Creates immediate cognitive dissonance — the brain wants to resolve the contradiction.
**Best for:** Cold traffic, Facebook/Instagram feeds, awareness-level 3-4 audiences who've tried the category before

**Formula:**
`Forget [mainstream solution] — there's a [smarter/simpler/natural] way to [desired outcome]`

**Real examples from winning ads:**

**Lever:** Fear + Curiosity
**What it does:** Opens with a direct command ("STOP," "WARNING," "READ THIS BEFORE YOU BUY") that triggers the reader's protective instincts. The implied threat creates urgency to process the message. Reads as consumer protection, not advertising.
**Best for:** Facebook/Instagram cold traffic, category comparison pages, review-style content, pre-sell advertorials

**Formula:**
`WARNING: Don't [do X] until you [know this / see this / read this].
[What they're about to waste / risk / miss]
[The specific information that would change their decision]`

OR:

`STOP [doing X that doesn't work].
[Here's what you should be doing instead]`

**Real examples from winning ads:**
1. Brand: SpoiledChild | Days active: var | "STOP WASTING MONEY ON HAIR CARE THAT DOESN'T WORK. SpoiledChild's A22 Hair + Scalp Serum isn't just a bandaid for your symptoms—it's an all-in-one solution to your hair care concerns."
2. Brand: Coffee We Love | Days active: 267 | "WARNING: Don't waste money on bad mushroom coffee! I tested + reviewed the internet's favorites for you!"
3. Brand: Coffee We Love | Days active: var | Title: "Read Before You Buy!" | Body: "Is Cuppa legit? I drank the viral mushroom coffee for 30 days straight to see what all the hype was about and the results were surprising!"

**Folqs/supplement adaptation:**
`WARNING: Don't buy another gut health supplement until you read this.
Most probiotic and gut supplements are tested in the wrong order.
They work on bacteria that's already broken through a compromised gut wall.
That's like mopping the floor while the pipe is still leaking.
First-day colostrum repairs the wall first.`

## HOOK SELECTION GUIDE

| Traffic Temperature | Best Hook Types |

| Cold (no awareness) | Contrarian/Pattern Interrupt, Direct Problem Callout, Science Myth-Bust, Curiosity Question, Warning/Stop-Scroll, **Humor/Fun** |
| Warm (problem aware) | Personal Story, Institution/Injustice, Avatar Callout, Comparison/Us vs. Them, Personal Test/Experiment, Authority Discovery |
| Hot (solution aware) | Offer-First/Discount, Benefit Stack, Weekly Transformation Timeline, Social Proof Scale, Winback |

**Format note:** Personal Story, Group Mirror, Institution/Injustice, and Avatar Callout are primarily effective in video and long-form advertorial. In static image ads, the top performers are Benefit Stack, Facts/Stats, Discount, and Testimonial.

**For Folqs Colostrum (organ supplement category):**

## HOOKS 18-20 — DISCOVERED FROM 3,849 VIDEO AD ANALYSIS (2026-03-06)

### HOOK TYPE 18: The Group Mirror
**Lever:** Identity / Belonging
**v5 frequency:** 0.6% of ads, 4 brands (niche — Suzy Cohen, Ayurveda Experience, 40 Plus & Fabulous)
**What it does:** Viewer self-selects into a collective "we" without being directly called out. No "YOU" — softer than Avatar Callout, bypasses defensiveness. Works by making the reader feel seen as part of a group, not targeted as an individual.
**Best for:** Broad cold audiences, older demographics (45+), health/wellness

**Formula:**
`"Some of us [specific behavior A]. Others of us [different version of same pain]. We're all [shared frustration with current solutions]. We all want [shared desire]."`

**Real examples from winning ads:**
1. Brand: Suzy Cohen | 969 days | "Some of us don't like to look in the mirror because of our weight, or our wrinkles, or our new grey hairs. Others of us have just stopped caring about how we look or feel. We're all tired of spending money on shots, expensive creams, and hair products galore. We all want to look more attractive and feel more energetic."
2. Brand: Suzy Cohen | 976 days | "When memory loss starts affecting daily life, it's frustrating and embarrassing. Everyone knows what I'm talking about — those senior moments that start coming more often."

**Folqs adaptation:**
`"Some of us spend $80 a month on five different supplements and still hit a wall by 2pm. Others of us have just given up on feeling the way we used to. We're all tired of products that promise everything and change nothing. We all want what our bodies were actually built to run on."`

### HOOK TYPE 19: Authority Discovery / "What If I Claimed..."
**Lever:** Curiosity + Authority
**v5 frequency:** 11.2% of ads, 35 brands (widely adopted — 7th most common hook type)
**What it does:** Opens with a rhetorical question that invites the viewer in, then delivers a softened but real claim, then introduces the authority figure AFTER curiosity is established. Authority arrives as the reward for staying — not as a credential upfront.
**Best for:** Cold traffic, skeptical audiences, anti-aging/longevity/performance claims

**Formula:**
`"What would you say if I claimed [extraordinary thing]? Well, [soften slightly], but [real version of the claim is still remarkable]. I'm [Authority Name/Title] and what I discovered..."`

**Real examples from winning ads:**
1. Brand: Stem Cell Renew | 1,580 days | Title: "The Shortcut To Fight Aging (Uncovered By a NASA Scientist)" | Body: "What would you say if I claimed that we could stay young forever? Well, forever may be a stretch, but I can tell you that cutting-edge research has shown there's a way for us to dramatically change the way we age. The secret isn't crazy exercise, strange potions, or even vitamins."

**Folqs adaptation:**
`"What would you say if I told you that one food — eaten by every high-performing human civilization in history — was quietly removed from the modern diet? Not by accident. By design. I'm [name] and when I found out what was missing, it changed everything about how I think about energy."`

### HOOK TYPE 20: Regret Prevention / "What If?"
**Lever:** Fear (of inaction) + Identity
**v5 frequency:** 1.8% of ads, 12 brands
**What it does:** Flips standard fear-based hooks. Instead of fearing the problem, the viewer fears their own future inaction. Identity-level consequence — "a year from now you'll wish you'd started today." Works because it's aspirational and threatening simultaneously.
**Best for:** Warm audiences, retargeting, decision-stage prospects

**Formula:**
`"[Name] took action because she didn't want to wake up [time period] later wondering 'what if?' As [Name] said: 'You WILL regret [inaction]. You won't regret [taking action].'" OR direct: "Don't wonder 'what if?' — [time from now] will come regardless. The only question is whether you'll be [outcome A] or [outcome B]."`

**Real examples from winning ads:**
1. Brand: Redefining Strength | 775 days | Title: "Don't wonder 'What if?' you'd taken action..." | Body: "'WHAT IF?!' Margarita took a leap into something new because she didn't want to wake up a year later wondering. 'Deciding to invest in myself was huge. You won't regret it. You WILL regret going back and seeing a year ago I had an opportunity to do this thing and I didn't do it.'"

**Folqs adaptation:**
`"Marcus decided to try Primal Eight because he didn't want to wake up next year still dragging through every Tuesday. As he put it: 'You'll regret not trying it. You won't regret finding out what you've been missing.' 90 days. Risk-free. Don't wait."`

### HOOK TYPE 21: Humor / Fun / Relatability
**Lever:** Pattern Interrupt + Desire (via positive emotion)
**v5 frequency:** 7.7% of ads, 34 brands (9th most common — more widely used than Warning, Discovery, or any niche hook)
**What it does:** Uses humor, memes, or relatable everyday scenarios to stop the scroll through positive emotion instead of fear or curiosity. The reader engages because the content feels like entertainment, not advertising. Humor ads have the highest shareability — they spread organically through saves and shares.
**Best for:** Cold traffic, broad audiences, brand awareness, categories where the product is fun/lifestyle-oriented (beverages, food, personal care, fitness). Less effective for serious medical/pain conditions.

**Formula:**
`[Relatable scenario everyone has experienced] + [Humorous twist that connects to the product]`

OR emoji-heavy meme format:
`😂 When you [relatable behavior]... but then you try [product] and [unexpected positive outcome]`

**Real patterns from v5 data:**
- Brands using humor most: BetterMe, Hungryroot, Dr. Squatch, Poppi, Olipop, MANSCAPED, Huel
- Often combined with: Benefit Stack (humor hook → benefit list body) or Testimonial (funny quote → product info)
- Works in image ads as text overlays, meme formats, and "reaction" style creative
- MANSCAPED and Dr. Squatch are the masters of this in men's grooming — irreverent, shareable, brand-building

**Folqs/supplement adaptation:**
`Your morning routine: Coffee. Emails. Bloating. Regret.
Their morning routine: Colostrum. Energy. No bloating. No regret.
Folqs first-day colostrum. Upgrade the routine.`

## VIDEO FORMAT INSIGHT (from 3,849 winning video ads)

**99.5% of winning Facebook/Instagram video ads are under 1 minute.**

This changes how to think about video copy:
- The 3-minute story VSL is for YouTube and long-form placements
- Facebook/Instagram winning format = 30-60 second cut
- Every 3-minute master script needs a 45-second Facebook-first cut (not just a 1-minute cut)
- Hook must work in 0-5 seconds — the first sentence is everything

**Recommended cut hierarchy:**
1. 3-min master (YouTube, landing page video, long-form)
2. 60-sec Facebook cut (primary paid social)
3. 45-sec Instagram cut (same as 60-sec but tighter)
4. 15-sec hook-only (for retargeting audiences)
