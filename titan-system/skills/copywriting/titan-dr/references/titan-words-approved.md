# TITAN WORDS — APPROVED POWER LIBRARY

> Words are the ammunition. This library is the armory.
> Every word earns its place or gets cut.

---

## CATEGORY 1: CURIOSITY & MYSTERY (Open Loops)

These words create information gaps the brain must resolve:

**Primary Curiosity Triggers:**
strange / unusual / odd / surprising / unexpected / hidden / secret / overlooked / unknown / forgotten / buried / discovered / reveals / uncovers / exposes / confesses / truth / real reason / what nobody tells you / what they don't want you to know / the one thing / the missing piece

**Curiosity Phrase Starters:**
- "The strange reason why..."
- "Why most [people] never..."
- "The overlooked [noun] that..."
- "What [authority] forgot to mention..."
- "The real reason [common belief] is wrong..."
- "Here's what's actually happening inside your..."

---

## CATEGORY 2: URGENCY & SCARCITY

Create genuine time/availability pressure:

**Urgency Words:**
now / today / immediately / tonight / this week / before it's gone / while supplies last / limited batch / last chance / expires / deadline / running out / only X left / selling fast / almost gone

**Scarcity Phrase Templates:**
- "Only [number] units from this [specific batch/harvest/production run]"
- "Once this batch is gone, it's gone — we source from [specific origin]"
- "We limit production to ensure [quality reason]"
- "This offer ends [specific date + specific reason]"

> **Rule:** Always give a reason for scarcity. Reason-based scarcity converts. Baseless scarcity triggers distrust.

## CATEGORY 3: AUTHORITY & CREDIBILITY

Build trust through expertise and validation:

**Authority Amplifiers:**
proven / verified / clinically studied / peer-reviewed / board-certified / award-winning / patented / trademarked / university-developed / doctor-formulated / recommended by / as seen in / according to / researchers found / published in / documented / on record

**Institutional Credibility Phrases:**
- "Dr. [Name], [specialty], [institution]"
- "In a [X]-participant study conducted by..."
- "Featured in [publication]"
- "[Number] doctors now recommend..."
- "Third-party tested and verified by [lab name]"

## CATEGORY 6: IDENTITY & TRIBE

Activate belonging, status, and self-concept:

**Identity Words:**
people like you / those who / for the [adjective] / the ones who / committed / serious / refuse to settle / done with / tired of / fed up / believers / real [noun] / the minority who / for those who know / forward-thinking / health-conscious / discerning / smart enough to

**Tribe-Building Templates:**
- "For the [X]% who refuse to [accept mediocre result]"

- "This isn't for everyone. It's for people who [specific qualifier]"
- "Join [number] people who decided [identity statement]"
- "The kind of person who [reads this / does this / thinks like this]..."

## CATEGORY 7: SPECIFICITY AMPLIFIERS

Make any claim more credible instantly:

**Number Patterns That Signal Authenticity:**
- Use odd, non-round numbers: "31,482" not "30,000" / "23.7%" not "24%"
- Add decimals to stats: "4.8 out of 5" not "5 stars"
- Specific timeframes: "in 72 hours" / "by Day 9" / "within the first 3 weeks"
- Specific money: "$127/month" not "expensive" / "$0.89 per day" not "affordable"
- Specific locations: "Sarasota, FL" / "sourced from New Zealand pasture farms"
- Specific dates/times: "2am on a Tuesday" / "six weeks ago" / "March 2024"

**Specificity Phrase Templates:**
- "In a survey of [specific number] customers, [X]% reported..."
- "The [specific organ/process] responds in as little as [specific time]..."
- "[Character name], [age], [specific location]: '[testimony]'"
- "At $[amount] per day — less than a latte..."

## CATEGORY 8: CTA VERBS & ACTION LANGUAGE

The final push language. Replace all weak CTA verbs:

**Weak CTAs (Never Use):**
- Submit / Click here / Buy now / Order / Purchase / Continue / Proceed

**Power CTA Verbs:**
Claim / Get / Unlock / Discover / Start / Try / Join / Access / Reserve / Grab / Take / Secure / Activate

**Power CTA Constructions:**
- "GET [DISCOUNT/OFFER] →"
- "CLAIM YOUR [PRODUCT] →"
- "TRY IT RISK-FREE →"
- "UNLOCK YOUR [TRANSFORMATION] →"
- "START YOUR [TIMEFRAME] TRIAL →"
- "SECURE [NUMBER] BOTTLE SUPPLY →"
- "RESERVE YOUR SPOT →"

## CATEGORY 9: TRANSITION & MOMENTUM PHRASES

Keep the reader moving. Prevent the page from going dead between sections.

**High-Velocity Transitions (Peter Kell):**
- "And then what happened was..."
- "The best part was..."
- "The craziest thing is..."
- "What happened next was..."
- "After that, everything changed..."
- "But here's where it gets really interesting..."
- "And that's when I realized..."
- "Which is why..."
- "So here's what we did..."
- "And the results were..."

**Section Bridge Phrases:**
- "Now that you understand [previous section], here's [next point]..."
- "So far you've seen [recap]. But here's what most people never find out..."
- "That brings us to the most important part..."
- "Which leads to the question everyone asks at this point..."

## CATEGORY 10: SENSORY & VISCERAL LANGUAGE

Make the copy physical. The brain processes sensory language as experience.

**Physical Sensation Words:**
tight / heavy / burning / bloated / foggy / dull / aching / sharp / drained / exhausted / sluggish / swollen / cramping / raw / hollow / leaking / sealed / smooth / clear / light / alive / sharp / focused / full / satisfied / warm / calm

**Transformation Before/After Sensory:**

| Before State | After State |
|-------------|-------------|
| "That constant bloated heaviness after every meal" | "Waking up with a flat, calm stomach" |
| "The brain fog that makes 3pm feel like midnight" | "Mental clarity that lasts through the afternoon" |
| "Dreading breakfast, knowing what's coming" | "Eating without the anxiety of what happens next" |
| "Never feeling empty, even when you haven't eaten" | "Actually feeling hungry — and satisfied after" |
| "Your stomach talking to you in the worst way" | "Quiet. Just... quiet." |

## CATEGORY 11: SOCIAL PROOF AMPLIFIERS

Make testimonials and numbers hit harder:

**Testimonial Lead-Ins:**
- "Here's what happened when [name] tried it:"
- "[Name], [age], [location]: 'I didn't believe it until...'"
- "The response that surprised us most:"
- "This is the kind of thing that makes what we do worth it:"

**Review Volume Phrases:**
- "[Number]+ five-star reviews" ← always use + suffix
- "9 out of 10 customers reorder"
- "[X]% saw results in their first 30 days"
- "Over [number] people have made the switch"

**Authority Social Proof:**
- "[Number] healthcare practitioners now recommend..."
- "Featured in [publications]"
- "Backed by [certification/testing body]"
