## FOLQS COLOSTRUM (BOVINE COLOSTRUM)

### Core Specs (verified from supplement-facts label, 2026-05-13)
- **Source:** First-day milking only (within 24 hours post-calving, when growth factors and IgG peak)
- **Active:** **ColosTRUE GOLD® Bovine Colostrum 2g per serving** — full-fat, non-skimmed
- **IgG content:** **420 mg per scoop (~21% by weight)** — printed on supplement-facts panel; verified by 3rd-party lab every batch
- **Other declared compounds (from pouch):** IgG · IgA · IgM · TGF-β · IGF-1 · IGF-2 · EGF
- **Serving:** 1 scoop = 2g · 60 servings/pouch (4.23 oz / 120g) · Dietary Supplement Unflavored
- **Pricing:** $79.99 one-time / $63.99 subscribe · 90-day money-back guarantee
- **Certifications (5):** Made in USA · GMP-Certified · NSF-Certified · FDA-Registered Facility · Lab-Tested
- **Other ingredients:** non-GMO sunflower lecithin · Contains: Milk
- **Mixing note:** avoid hot/warm liquids (preserves bioactives)

### The Key Differentiators
1. **First-day milking** (not multi-day pooled — most competitors blend Day 1–4+ outputs)
2. **Full-fat, non-skimmed** (Armra is "NO FAT" per their own label — strips the fat-soluble bioactive layer)
3. **420 mg IgG declared on the label** (most competitors don't print theirs)
4. **NSF-Certified** (Armra does not carry NSF)
5. **90-day no-questions-asked guarantee** (Armra does not match)

### Flagship study (use as anchor citation like Marchbank 2011 anchors colostrum)
**Mortensen 2014 Q-SYMBIO trial** (PMID 25282031) — 420 patients, 2yr RCT of CoQ10 in heart failure:
- MACE: 15% (CoQ10) vs 26% (placebo), HR 0.50, p=0.003
- All-cause mortality: 10% vs 18%, p=0.018
- Beef heart = richest natural CoQ10 source (113–192 µg/g per Latoch 2024)

### Compliance edge — claims to SOFTEN, not state
- **Thymus** — Cochrane 2011 (Wolf): zero RCT support for thymus powder. Frame as "ancestral tradition / signaling peptides studied" — NO efficacy claim
- **Lung** — no oral supplementation RCT exists. Frame as "ancestral eighth / nose-to-tail completeness" — NO health-outcome claim
- **Spleen** — glandular "immune peptide" narrative is weak. Lead with heme iron (Valenzuela 2009: 72.8% heme fraction, highest of any organ)
- **Bone Marrow** — NEVER say "stem cells" (dried powder has zero viable cells, FTC-risky). Frame as glycine/proline/collagen precursors + fatty acids
- **Men 40+ angles** — use "vitality" / "stamina" / "energy you had at 30" — NEVER testosterone
