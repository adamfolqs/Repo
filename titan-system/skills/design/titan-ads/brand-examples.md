# Titan Ads — Brand Examples

> Annotated ad creative breakdowns from 13 DTC brands in our FOTW research.
> Each entry: what works, visual style, copy approach, performance data.

## 1. ColonBroom (50 ads, 61d avg winning)

**Category**: Gut health / fiber supplement / GLP-1 booster
**Visual Style**: Purple-dominant (#2D1B4E), modern, clinical-but-approachable
**Winning Patterns Used**: Product Hero (#2), Testimonial Billboard (#6), Dark Mode (#15)

**Top Creative**: "TIRED OF BEING FAT." — purple gradient, 3 GLP-1 jars
- Pattern: Product Hero + Bold Claim
- Why it works: Provocative headline stops scroll, product centered, clean layout
- Disclaimer at bottom: "Along with a balanced diet and regular physical activity..."

**Top Creative**: Delivery truck billboard — testimonial on moving truck
- Pattern: Testimonial Billboard
- Why it works: Implies scale, real customer quote, product visible
- Quote: "I've lost 12 lbs in just 4 weeks with GLP-1 Booster! 💜"

**Brand Rules**: Always includes weight loss disclaimer. Purple brand color. "ColonBroom" wordmark at top.

## 2. Folqs (18 ads, 90d avg winning)

**Category**: First-day colostrum supplement
**Visual Style**: Dark gold gradients, premium, warm tones, science-forward
**Winning Patterns Used**: Us vs Them (#9), Mechanism Explainer (#12), Problem-State (#1)

**Top Creative**: "YOUR $100 COLOSTRUM IS LITERAL ANIMAL FEED"
- Pattern: Us vs Them + Mechanism Explainer (hybrid)
- Dark-to-gold gradient background, glass of colostrum powder
- 3 icon callout bubbles explaining first-day vs day-2 milk
- Bottom: "FIRST-DAY COLOSTRUM IS GOLDEN. FOLQS IS GOLDEN."
- Why it works: Directly attacks competitor positioning, educates simultaneously

**Brand Rules**: Golden/warm color palette. "First-day" messaging is core differentiator. Premium positioning — never discount-led.

## 4. Derila (47 ads, 68d winning)

**Category**: Sleep pillow / snoring solution
**Visual Style**: White background with illustration, cartoon-style hooks
**Winning Patterns Used**: Illustrated Hook (#4)

**Top Creative**: Cartoon man sleeping with drool
- Pattern: Illustrated Hook
- White background, bold black text hook on top, cartoon below
- "Most People with Sleep Apnea don't use this to stop snoring, but did you know... more"
- Why it works: Curiosity gap ("...more"), illustration is non-threatening, pattern interrupt

**Brand Rules**: Uses illustration to bypass Meta's body-image restrictions. Curiosity-gap headlines. Targets snoring/sleep apnea indirectly.

## Cross-Brand Patterns

### What Winning Brands Have in Common
1. **Consistent visual identity** — same colors, fonts, layout across all ads
2. **One message per creative** — never try to say everything in one ad
3. **Product is always visible** (except problem-state ads)
4. **Social proof somewhere** — in image, body copy, or headline
5. **Clear CTA** — SHOP_NOW (66%) or LEARN_MORE (30%)

### What Differentiates #1 Performers
1. **Mechanism education** — Folqs, Forefront Health explain HOW it works
2. **Emotional scroll-stoppers** — Primal Viking, Folqs problem-state photos
3. **Story-format ads** — Javvy, text-heavy cards that feel like content not ads
4. **UGC at scale** — Provitalize, ARMRA use creator networks exclusively
